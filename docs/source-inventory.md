Status meanings:

- **Adapted:** a public teaching edition of the source's concepts and relevant technical details has been published. It may be condensed or anonymized; **not necessarily an exact or complete transcription**.
- **Partially adapted:** only selected sections/examples are public; remaining content may be reviewed separately.
- **Concepts covered:** the public site reflects its general methodology, but no one-to-one replacement file exists.
- **Pending / Review:** preparation or rights/content review is still needed.
- **Historical / Metadata:** the source explains how the archive was maintained; current release notes are managed directly in GitHub.
