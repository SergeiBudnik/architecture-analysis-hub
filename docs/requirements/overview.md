# Requirements & Analysis

The source library organizes this workstream into:
- **BPMN**: process modeling and process-flow examples.
- **Requirements documentation**: BRD, business requirements, functional/non-functional requirements and SRS.
- **Use cases**: scenario descriptions, examples and templates.
- **User stories**: user-centered requirements and examples.

A practical workflow is to capture the business goal, define scope and constraints, identify functional and non-functional requirements, then refine behaviors into scenarios and testable specifications.

*Source: author's requirements collection. This page is an introductory editorial guide, not a reproduction of the original templates.*
