# Community Use and Independent Feedback

This is a **public, evidence-based log** for the reuse of Architecture & Analysis Hub materials in professional education, independent study, architecture reviews and open technical collaboration.

The purpose is to make the library easier to improve and to document real outcomes **without inventing statistics or testimonials**.

## Current status

**Verified third-party use cases published: 0** (initial register, 18 October 2026).

**Verified testimonials published: 0** (initial register, 18 October 2026).

GitHub stars, page visits and post reactions are **not** treated as proof that someone has actually used the resources. An issue report or a message alone is a lead for review, not automatically a verified adoption case.

An entry is added only when a real independent user provides sufficient context and **explicitly agrees** to a public factual description. The maintainer records a link to public evidence or a non-sensitive, permissioned confirmation. We do not claim an organization endorses this resource unless it has expressly done so.

## What counts as a useful report?

- **Educational use:** An instructor uses one of the examples during an actual workshop, lecture or practical exercise; they can optionally share a public agenda or course page.
- **Professional use:** An analyst or architect adapted a template or diagram and can describe a concrete design-review outcome without revealing proprietary details.
- **Open contribution:** A third-party pull request, issue discussion or independently authored article improves or cites an actual resource.
- **Thoughtful criticism:** Technical corrections are valuable even if the contributor has not used the content in a professional setting; they belong in the feedback queue, **not** in the verified adoption table.

## Share your experience (optional)

Use the [GitHub issue chooser](https://github.com/SergeiBudnik/architecture-analysis-hub/issues/new/choose) and select **Share a real use of a resource**.

Please tell us which public page or diagram you used, in what type of activity (for example a training exercise or system-design review), what changed or what was learned, and an optional public link if available. You can use "independent practitioner" instead of naming an employer.

We will ask for **separate permission** before quoting your words or attributing your organization in the register. You can share a useful correction without agreeing to public attribution.

**Do not submit:** client specifications, employee/customer names, unpublished projects, financial identifiers, PAN/CVV, credentials, restricted scheme documents or internal architecture diagrams.

## How the maintainer validates an entry

1. Check that the referenced resource exists and the external contribution is specific to it.
2. Confirm that the use actually happened, rather than merely being planned.
3. Identify a permissioned public source, or request a short independently authored confirmation that reveals no protected information.
4. Record only the minimum factual statement and evidence link, and add the verification date.
5. Remove or anonymize an entry if its contributor withdraws consent or the source can no longer be verified.

**No fake endorsements, commissioned positive reviews, reciprocal testimonial trades or self-reported metrics presented as independently verified evidence.**
